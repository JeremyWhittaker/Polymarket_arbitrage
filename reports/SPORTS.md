# Completion of the twelve selected sports hypotheses

This maps the football/soccer/baseball research program requested after Claude's earlier structural screens. The corrected soccer full run has passed independent cash, clock, capacity and native-action checks. The selected definitions were recovered from the original session; this is not a substitute list of new winning ideas. Historical July–September evaluation data had already been inspected. The [viability assessment](VIABILITY.md) ranks the evidence across the whole project.

All twelve primary transaction studies are implemented. The final original-protocol audit also restored NRFI’s requested equal-game confidence interval, separately from cash-weighted results. The first full soccer run covered 4,739 games / 106,519 events and passed cash checks, but a subsequent raw-action audit found that complement SELLs violated the explicit taker-BUY protocol. Those results are archived and superseded. Actual BUY entry/detection and SELL unwind are repaired; 49 soccer/engine tests pass. The corrected full 38-configuration rerun passed all 152 summary checks, tracing 50,491 entry/exit records back to raw actions, tokens, prices and sizes.

Two original algebraic claims required correction. The baseball pair No(home−1.5)+No(away−1.5) pays one plus a one-run bonus, so implied one-run mass is **pair price minus one**, not two minus price. The football secondary No(home moneyline)+Yes(home by 1.5) pays **zero** on a one-point home win; it cannot be called arbitrage. Neither error was silently converted into a different profitable rule.

All twelve original primary transaction rules have code and regression coverage; the NRFI inference supplement was accepted with unchanged economic outputs. Invalid original execution assumptions were replaced explicitly: future observations cannot set a past signal, observing a trade does not let us fill it retroactively, and a partial pair cannot be treated as a complete hedge. Soccer has 38 declared configurations with fee-only and +1¢ cases; results are correlated sensitivities, not 38 independent discoveries. The soccer report names optional variants not run and the specific missing depth/receipt data. Those limitations are kept separate from completed primary transaction tests.

Every accepted primary has a complete canonical signal/leg ledger, including zero-capital rejections and no fills. Candidate counts, leg counts and funded games therefore differ. The private desk also exposes the existing inning-discount grid and all 50 one-percent thresholds; overlapping ledgers are separate counterfactual policies, not a combined portfolio. Full input/code identities and actual cash reconciliation accompany the [validation evidence](REPAIR_VALIDATION.md).

The older structural screens in [THORP.md](THORP.md) remain archived research. Their existing negative/disputed results were reviewed, but this continuation does not claim a new received-book validation of every legacy maker strategy. No real-money orders were placed.
